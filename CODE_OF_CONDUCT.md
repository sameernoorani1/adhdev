# Code of Conduct

ADHDev follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Short version:

- Be kind. Assume the person on the other side is doing their best.
- No harassment, no personal attacks, no discrimination.
- Disagreement on technical decisions is welcome. Disagreement on someone's right to participate is not.

If something here makes you uncomfortable — in an issue, a PR, a Discord channel, or anywhere else under the ADHDev name — contact **sameernoorani@gmail.com** directly. Reports are read by Sameer and handled privately.

Bad behaviour can get you warned, then blocked from the repo or community spaces. We'd rather not do that; please don't make us.
