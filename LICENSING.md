# Licensing

ADHDev is **dual-licensed**.

## Open source: AGPL-3.0-or-later

The default license is [AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE). You can:

- Run ADHDev on your own machine, free, forever.
- Read the source, modify it, and share your modifications.
- Use ADHDev internally at your company.

The one rule: if you offer ADHDev (or a modified version) as a hosted service to other people, you have to release your modifications under the same license. That's the "Affero" part of AGPL — it's what keeps a hosting company from forking ADHDev, hosting it as a paid service, and pocketing the work without sharing improvements back.

For the overwhelming majority of users — individuals, indie builders, small teams running it locally — this never comes up.

## Commercial license

If AGPL doesn't fit (e.g., you want to embed ADHDev into a proprietary product, redistribute it under different terms, or host it as part of a closed-source SaaS), a commercial license is available.

Contact: **commercial@adhdev.app** (until that alias is live, open a GitHub Issue tagged `commercial-license` and we'll route it).

Sameer Noorani is the sole copyright holder and can grant commercial terms on a per-customer basis.

## Why dual licensing?

It keeps ADHDev free for everyone who can live with AGPL (most users), while giving companies that genuinely need different terms a clean path to get them. This pattern is used by Plausible Analytics, Cal.com, Grafana, and Sentry. It's not a trick — it's the explicit deal.
